🎯 What is a Goal?
A Goal is a larger result you want to achieve. A Task is an action that moves the Goal forward, and a Plan gives an activity a date and time.

Progress calculation
Completed linked Tasks ÷ Total linked Tasks × 100. Example: 3 completed out of 5 linked Tasks = 60%.
